Verification Workflow

Every uploaded media goes through multiple explainable AI verification stages before generating a transparent trust report.

01

Upload Evidence

Upload an image, video or audio file securely. Every file is encrypted and assigned a unique digital fingerprint before analysis begins.

02

AI Analysis

Multiple AI detection engines examine the uploaded media for GAN artifacts, manipulation traces, metadata inconsistencies and synthetic patterns.

03

Explainability

Every prediction is supported with confidence values, highlighted evidence regions and reasoning generated through explainable AI.

04

Trust Report

Generate a professional investigation report including Trust Score, confidence level, classification and complete evidence summary.

Core AI Modules

Trustify AI combines several intelligent verification modules instead of relying on a single prediction model.

Image Forensics

Detect AI-generated images, edited photographs, compression artifacts, cloning traces, lighting inconsistencies and hidden manipulation patterns.

Video Verification

Analyze frame consistency, temporal artifacts, lip synchronization, GAN fingerprints and deepfake manipulation indicators.

Audio Analysis

Detect cloned voices, synthetic speech, frequency anomalies and AI-generated voice characteristics.

Metadata Intelligence

Extract EXIF metadata, timestamps, GPS information, editing history and hidden forensic clues.

Explainable AI

Understand why Trustify AI reached a decision through confidence maps, evidence highlighting and feature importance.

Trust Score Engine

Calculate a transparent Trust Score by combining outputs from multiple forensic and AI verification engines.
