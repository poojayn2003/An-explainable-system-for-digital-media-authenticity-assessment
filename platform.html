METHOD

Our verification process

Every file moves through the same six stages, in the same order, before a verdict is ever shown.

01

Evidence

The file is uploaded, encrypted, and assigned a unique digital fingerprint.

02

Metadata Analysis

EXIF data, timestamps, and encoding history are read for tampering signs.

03

AI Artifact Detection

Forensic models scan for GAN fingerprints, splice traces, and synthetic patterns.

04

Explainability Engine

Every signal is translated into a plain-language reason, not just a number.

05

Trust Score

All signals are fused into a single 0–100 score reflecting overall confidence.

06

Investigation Report

A complete, downloadable report is generated with the full evidence trail.
